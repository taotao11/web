# Product Brief — 乱世赵匡胤：黄袍之前

## Promise
让玩家不是“阅读赵匡胤的历史”，而是在五代十国的饥荒、战争与权力缝隙中，以赵匡胤的身份承担每一个选择的代价。

## Format
Browser-first narrative game. Chinese UI. Desktop-first, responsive mobile layout.

## Experience
Narrative-led interactive fiction with hidden causal tags, character attitudes, branching scenes, failure, autosave, manual save slots, death recovery.

## Tone
冷峻、克制、沉浸、历史感。惨状通过环境、后果、人物选择呈现，不靠廉价血腥堆砌。

## Historical anchors
- 954: Later Zhou Emperor Shizong's accession and the Battle of Gaoping
- 955–959: Later Zhou consolidation and campaigns
- 960: Chenqiao Mutiny and founding of Song

## First slice
The player is placed in a starving military camp. A missing grain convoy, frightened civilians and a suspicious officer force the player to decide what kind of commander Zhao will become.

## Non-goals
No gacha. No combat minigame in v0.1. No visible stat spreadsheet. No AI-generated text at runtime.
